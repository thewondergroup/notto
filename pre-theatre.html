Good to know

Pre-theatre, answered.

Is there a pre-theatre menu?

Yes. Supperclub runs every day from 5pm at both restaurants: two courses for £19.50, three for £24.50. The full menu is on too if you'd rather.

Will we make the show?

Fresh pasta is fast food in the best sense. Allow 45 minutes to an hour, tell us your curtain time when you book, and we'll make sure you're out with time to find your seats.

Do we need to book?

We'd recommend it between 5 and 7pm, and especially at weekends. Book online and add the show and curtain time to the notes, or call the restaurant.

What about after the show?

Kitchens close at 9pm Monday to Saturday and 8pm on Sunday, so we're a before-the-show place rather than an after. Matinee crowd, come for Lunchissimo instead: a pasta, salad and focaccia for £14.85, midday to 5pm.

Larger groups?

Theatre trips with the office, a birthday, a school party that's outgrown pizza: private dining and group bookings are very much a thing.
