The NOTTO story

Exceptional pasta. For everyone.

NOTTO was founded by Michelin-starred chef Phil Howard around a simple idea: to make the kind of pasta he loves to cook, using the ingredients he believes in, at prices that mean you can eat it whenever you like.

Made fresh daily

Simple when it should be. Luxurious when it wants to be.

We make our pasta fresh every day and cook it to order, with sauces that run from beautifully simple tomato and basil to slow-cooked ragùs and richer, more indulgent things. Seasonal, generous and full of flavour.

Rule No.1

Never compromise on the ingredients.

Good cooking starts with good produce. We work with suppliers Phil has trusted for years, sourcing the best meat, vegetables, flour, cheese and Italian ingredients we can find. We spend where it matters, keep everything else simple, and make seriously good food at a price that makes sense.
