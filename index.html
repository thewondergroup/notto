The NOTTO story

To send you back into the world a happier person, having eaten some exceptional pasta.

Take one of Britain's most acclaimed chefs, add his lifelong love of pasta — a dish that has always featured on Phil Howard's Michelin-starred menus — and the result is NOTTO.

Made fresh daily

Sauced from rustically simple to super luxurious.

We make our pasta every day and serve it with exceptional sauces. Seasonal, satisfying, and made from the very best ingredients — which doesn't mean expensive. Simple, affordable, and very, very good.

Rule No.1

Only the finest ingredients.

Above all else, we never — ever — compromise on the quality of the ingredients we use. Great food that's as good for you as it is for the planet takes carefully selected produce. There's no shortcut, and we don't look for one.
