About

Two concepts. One family.

NOTTO exists in two formats — each made for a different kind of moment, both sharing the same soul. The same chefs. The same recipes. The same ingredient obsession. One is made for slowing down. The other is built for speeding up.

The aim

To send you back into the world a happier person, having eaten some exceptional pasta.

Take one of Britain's most acclaimed chefs, add his love for pasta — a dish that has always featured on Phil Howard's Michelin-starred menus — and the result is NOTTO. We make our pasta daily and serve it with exceptional sauces, from rustically simple to super luxurious. Seasonal, satisfying, and made from the very best ingredients. That doesn't mean expensive — it's simple, affordable, and very, very good.

Rule No.1

Only the finest ingredients.

Above all else, we never — ever — compromise on the quality of the ingredients we use. Great food that's as good for you as it is for the planet takes carefully selected produce. There's no shortcut, and we don't look for one.
